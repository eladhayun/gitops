Private padel booking assistant

Wednesday Club

An owner-operated service that monitors padel availability and manages approved bookings through Lazuz.

Google Calendar integration

After the owner authorizes Google access and explicitly selects an owned calendar, Wednesday Club can add private events for future confirmed bookings. Events contain booking details and one popup reminder 60 minutes before play. No guests are invited.

Verified booking cancellations can remove the matching event. Existing bookings are not backfilled, and existing iCloud events are not modified.

Owner-controlled access

This is a personal service managed by Elad Hayun, not a public booking platform. There is no public sign-up. Calendar authorization does not authorize a purchase or enable booking automation.

Wednesday Club is independent of Google and Lazuz. Booking availability, charges and provider cancellation rules remain subject to the booking provider.
