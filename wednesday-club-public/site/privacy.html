Privacy Policy

Wednesday Club is a personal padel booking assistant operated by Elad Hayun. Google access is used only for the owner's explicitly enabled Calendar integration.

Google data we access

With the owner's consent, the service accesses the Google account identifier and verified email, metadata for calendars the owner owns, and event data needed to create, verify, reconcile or remove its booking events in the selected calendar. It does not request Gmail messages, contacts or Drive files.

Google account and calendar identifiers are pinned to the selected destination. The service does not use a default calendar as a fallback, invite attendees, or treat incoming messages as booking commands.

Use and sharing

Google data is used to verify the authorized account, list eligible calendars and manage booking events. It is not sold, used for advertising, used to train generalized AI models, or shared with message recipients.

Google processes Calendar API requests. The service runs on Microsoft Azure; access-restricted storage holds authorization and delivery records. Encrypted deployment material is stored in the owner's GitHub GitOps repository. These providers process data as needed to operate the service.

Separately enabled Slack or WhatsApp notifications use booking information and their approved destinations. Google authorization does not enable those integrations or authorize sharing.

Wednesday Club's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are kept in private, access-restricted files and encrypted deployment material. They are not exposed through public pages or MCP tools. Google account/calendar bindings and immutable event-delivery evidence are retained to prevent duplicate writes and support reconciliation.

Historical booking, financial and audit evidence may be retained for accounting, safety and dispute review. Backups may retain earlier records until the configured retention period expires. Credentials and records are not intentionally included in public logs.

Control and deletion

The owner can disable Calendar delivery in Wednesday Club and revoke access through Google Account permissions. Disabling delivery does not itself revoke a token or delete existing events. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not erase stored evidence or events already created.

Contact elad.hayun@gmail.com to request credential removal or deletion of data. Requests are reviewed against necessary financial and safety record retention. The service does not delete unrelated calendar events.

Public pages and changes

These information pages use no analytics scripts, advertising or application cookies. Hosting, ingress and network providers may process request metadata such as IP addresses for delivery and security.

Material changes to these practices will be reflected here with an updated effective date. Questions can be sent to the contact address above.
